Software Developer

Currently I am most interested in software development. Software development as defined by the Bureau of Labor Stastics, as analyst, testers, and developers involved in creating a software program. They themselves do not have to be programmers, though they can be, but they do work closely with programmers to make sure the program meets certain criteria. They also perform routine maintenance on their programs.

There are many types of jobs in the field such as :

  • Software Engineers
  • Application Developers
  • System Developers

Job Requirements

Both the Bureau of Labor Statistics and Computer Science.org suggest that Software Developers typically need a Bachelor's degree in an IT field. Depending on the job too, the programmer must be familiar with a variety of programming languages and keep up to date with new ones. Computer Science.org goes into more detail, reccomending an understanding Data structure and Security. This is because certain Software Developers must also be analyst, understanding how to store and keep data secure. Most Software Developers also start their careers in internships while others get experience through coding bootcamps.

Software Developers also find the following traits useful:

  • Having problem solving skills
  • Being communicative with colleagues
  • Being detail observant and oreinted
